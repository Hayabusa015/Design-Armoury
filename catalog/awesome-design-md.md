## Design character
Describes the design systems of real products in a fixed nine-section format: visual theme, color palette, typography, components, layout, depth, do's and don'ts, responsive behavior, and an agent prompt guide. Skews toward developer tools, AI platforms, fintech, and modern SaaS. Not verified: accuracy of any individual file.

## Best for
Learning how a respected product structures its look, borrowing a documented approach (spacing, type scale, depth cues) as inspiration, and as a model for writing the project's own DESIGN.md.

## Avoid when
The goal is a distinctive brand of the user's own, or when the result would read as a copy of another company's identity. Do not clone a brand's logo, name, or distinctive look.

## What's inside
A folder per site with a DESIGN.md plus `preview.html` and `preview-dark.html` catalog pages. Groups include AI and LLM platforms, developer tools, backend and DevOps, productivity and SaaS, design and creative tools, fintech and crypto, e-commerce, media, automotive, and retro web. The README lists the full set.

## How to use
Read a relevant DESIGN.md for ideas, or copy one into a project root as a starting reference and tell the agent to follow it. Prefer writing an original DESIGN.md informed by several of them.

## Gotchas
- Files are analyses of public CSS and pages, provided as-is and possibly out of date.
- Matching a living brand closely can raise trademark and trade-dress concerns for client work.
- Many entries skew dark and developer-tool in style, which may not fit friendly or print-oriented projects.

## Overlaps and pairings
Complements component entries: this says what a system should look like, those supply the parts. Use it to inform the Draftsman's blueprint tokens, not to replace them.

## Not verified yet
