# 21st.dev glass and scroll picks

What it is: A shortlist of community components on 21st.dev found by searching for glass and scroll-reveal looks. Each is a separate page by a different author.

Why I like it: I like all of them.

Steal this:
- Glass Fold (https://21st.dev/@meerbahadin10/components/glass-fold, MIT): an animated WebGL background of translucent glass panes folded over each other, lit at the creases, for behind a hero or card. (observed)
- Glass Blog Card (https://21st.dev/@moumensoliman/components/glass-blog-card-shadcnui, MIT): frosted card with an image zoom, built on framer-motion. (observed)
- Scroll Reveal Image (https://21st.dev/@unlumen/components/scroll-reveal-image, MIT): the image widens, zooms out, and rounds its corners with spring physics as it enters view. The closest match to the scroll feel described in Philosophy.md. (observed)
- Glass Calendar (https://21st.dev/@ravikatiyar162/components/glass-calendar): glass calendar with smooth horizontal scrolling through the days. License listed as unknown.
- Liquid Glass (https://21st.dev/@suraj-xd/components/liquid-glass): liquid glass over a moving background with Dock-style icons. License listed as no-license.
- Liquid Glass Card (https://21st.dev/@designali-in/components/liquid-glass-card) and Apple Tahoe Liquid Glass Button (https://21st.dev/@jahed/components/apple-tahoe-liquid-glass-button): glass card and button; licenses not checked.
- Smooth Scroll (https://21st.dev/@uilayout.contact/components/smooth-scroll) and Parallax Scrolling (https://21st.dev/@osmosupply/components/parallax-scrolling): found by name only; contents and licenses not checked.

Avoid:
- Do not copy any component whose license is unknown, no-license, or unchecked. Use those as look-and-feel references only.
- 21st.dev terms: copying a component to another site needs a visible link back to its original page, and their demos and media are not included in a component's license.
- Keep body text off glass and test contrast over the busiest backdrop. Refraction effects belong on one hero element, with a solid fallback for reduced transparency (21st.dev's own liquid-glass article).

Observed: From each component's 21st.dev page text only (description, listed license, dependencies). None of the previews were seen rendered. Install is through the shadcn CLI with a 21st.dev API key, with a daily limit on free accounts.
