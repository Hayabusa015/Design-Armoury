# Settigation animation packs

What it is: A storefront for paid UI animation packs. Its own page is built around the idea of "motion with restraint." The product files cost money and carry no-resale terms.

Why I like it: not given. It was studied for its animation style while planning a class website.

Steal this:
- The principle of motion with restraint: a fade plus a small upward translate, slow easing, and staggered delays for scroll reveals
- Small hover states on buttons and cards instead of big effects

Avoid:
- Do not extract or recreate the paid animation files. Reuse the principles only, written as original code.

Observed: Read from the page's markup in an earlier session. Class names such as `an-rise` and `in` suggest an IntersectionObserver-style scroll reveal. The demos are autoplaying muted videos that could not be analysed, and the stylesheet was not fetched, so exact timing, easing, and distances are not known.
