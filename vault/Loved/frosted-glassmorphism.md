# Frosted glassmorphism

What it is: A frosted-glass panel over a blurred, colourful background, shown in a stock mockup preview. It stands for the user's main design direction.

Why I like it: I am a big fan of frosted glassmorphism, and that clear glass look with opacity where you can see into the background, with a nice little motion and bounce on my websites and apps, and a nice scroll feel, so that when you scroll, stuff kind of wheels up and appears. That is kind of the main idea of how I like things to look.

Steal this:
- A translucent panel with a soft background blur and a thin light edge, over a rich background it can refract (observed)
- Light, wide-tracked uppercase headlines on the glass, with one brighter highlight strip behind a key label (observed)
- A little spring and bounce on interactions, and content that rises into place on scroll
- A fine grain over the glass for a frosted feel (observed)

Avoid:
- Do not reuse the stock preview image or its logo. It is watermarked and licensed.
- Check text contrast against the blurred backdrop. Glass over a busy background can fail readability.
